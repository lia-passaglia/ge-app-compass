# corporate reclamation playbook

When underutilized licenses are detected (active seats < 40%), follow these standardized administrative guidelines:

1. **notification phase**:
   - Send an automated friendly nudge email to managers of departments with utilization under 40%.
   - Offer a 7-day grace period for users to activate or complete their basic onboarding.
2. **reclamation phase**:
   - If after 7 days utilization remains below the threshold, automatically unassign inactive accounts via the Admin SDK / Admin Console.
   - Pool reclaimed licenses into the "Global Corporate Holding" unit.
3. **onboarding phase**:
   - Reassign reclaimed licenses to users on the "Onboarding Queue" (Engineering & Sales waitlists).
